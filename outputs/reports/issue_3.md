# Bug Report  
**Title:**  
> Payment not processed – users are charged multiple times (duplicate “froad” charges)

**Feature Area:** `payment_gateway`  

**Priority Score:** **0.70** (High – 122.5 × baseline error rate)  

**Review Count:** 18  

---

## Description  

Users are reporting that their payments are **not** being processed correctly. Instead of a single successful charge, the app sometimes creates **duplicate charges** or fails to reflect a successful transaction in the order history. The error rate is now **122.5 ×** the baseline, indicating a systemic problem in the payment flow
